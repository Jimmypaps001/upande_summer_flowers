{{ _("Geometry: bed area, spacing, and yield") }}

{{ _("Three inputs, and they do different jobs. Plants per bed is not typed in — it is derived, because three numbers where two will do is three numbers that can disagree.") }}

plants per bed = bed area (m² net) × density (plants per m²)
plants needed  = stems wanted ÷ stems per plant at that flush
beds           = ceil(plants needed ÷ plants per bed)
plants         = beds × plants per bed
ground         = beds × bed area

{{ _("Read that table before reporting a bug") }}

  • {{ _("Bigger beds do add plants per bed") }} — {{ _("50 m² × 20 = 1,000; 100 m² × 20 = 2,000. That part works as you would expect.") }}
  • {{ _("But the plan's total plants does not change") }}, {{ _("because the plan sizes to stems, not to area. Bigger beds mean fewer of them for the same crop.") }}
  • {{ _("Spacing moves the ground, not the plant count.") }} {{ _("Planting twice as tight grows the same plants in half the space.") }}
  • {{ _("Stems per plant is the only one that moves the plant count.") }} {{ _("Halve the yield and you need twice the plants — that is the number to change if a plan looks the wrong size.") }}
